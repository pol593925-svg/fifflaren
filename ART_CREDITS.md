# Graphics assets

Map architecture, operators, articulated gloves and weapon meshes were authored
for this project using the reproducible Blender scripts in `tools/blender`.
These assets are an original interpretation; they are not extracted Valve assets.

Surface photographs are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), under
[CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Powered by Poly Haven.
The game uses packaged files and does not call Poly Haven at runtime.

`models/csgo/textures/sources.json` records download URLs, channels and MD5 checksums.
Scans are supplied at 1024px; the Inferno wall albedo is tinted ochre in Blender.
Other surface detail, sky and clothing textures are generated by the included scripts.
Existing assets outside `models/csgo` predate this graphics update.

## Nature used in 3.44.1

Original Quaternius Ultimate Stylized Nature Pack (CC0): https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatestylizednature.html . Same source models as the Evolution prototype. Oak, birch, pine, flowering bush and rock are normalized and exported by tools/blender/build_nature.py, with 512/1024px textures. models/csgo/nature.glb is the compact runtime asset; its license is included alongside it. Terrain shader, rolling ridges and grass batching are original Support Hub code.

## Coast CS update 5.1.0

The three menu backgrounds in `assets/cs16/menu` are original AI-generated illustrations produced for this project, inspired by the owner's Mediterranean village reference. They are menu artwork, not screenshots of the playable renderer. The rotating arsenal uses the actual game models.

`tools/blender/build_coast_nature.py` replaces the earlier runtime nature pack with original olive, poplar, pine, shrub and limestone geometry. Bark and stone use the CC0 scans listed above, resized for the pooled runtime asset (under 2 MB). Historical Quaternius licensing files are retained for older assets.

`tools/blender/coast_architecture.py` bakes the shared building collision layout into the map with limestone trim, shutters, tiled eaves, entry awnings, balcony coping and planters. New M4 receiver/stock/handguard, carved AK/Galil stocks and curved magazines, articulated gloves and sleeves are original Blender geometry. The holographic optic is original Three.js geometry with an open aperture.

## Каменный Берег

The Battle Royale facade and street atlases are original generated artwork. The city also reuses the Poly Haven surface scans credited above. The transport plane, canopy, MP7, Vector and Bizon were modeled with the included Blender script.

## Local painted CS map review (unpublished)

The complete Dust2 and Inferno replacements in `models/csgo/anime-*.glb` are exported by `tools/blender/build_anime_maps.py`, `build_anime_weapons.py` and `build_anime_nature.py`. They use the shared CS16TacticalMap compact two-site layouts and original Support Hub weapon/hand rigs. tools/blender/anime_architecture.py authors the curved roof wings, capitals, doorway surrounds, shutters and ceramic planters over the shared collision model. Weapon housings have rounded edges; the anatomical hand surfaces are subdivided before export. Curved eaves, arched facade details, broad foliage, sleeve inlays and receiver engravings are authored geometry. The plaster and paving textures in `assets/cs-anime` are original AI-generated artwork from the approved courtyard prototype. All other colours, texture washes and the distant tree atlas are generated by the included authoring/runtime code. No Genshin or Overwatch assets are included.

The second local polish pass adds original scalloped textile awnings, wisteria, timber framing, curved balcony brackets, wooden stair nosings and rear garden-wall crests. Facade palette washes are baked into the images before glTF export. Sleeves retain their woven normal detail and receive curved bracers; the ivory, teal and brass reflex sight is batched while preserving its independent lens and reticle.

The third local pass rounds architectural trim, models arched window surrounds and three-dimensional balcony scrolls, and adds curved flower petals and ceramic medallions. Fitted forearm plates, stitched piping and blue enamel receiver panels with brass engravings are original authored geometry. No new external art sources are used.

The fourth local pass adds barrel-tile roof relief, carved side cornices, cloth guild banners, moulded interactive door leaves and brass ring handles. Cuff embroidery, glove knuckle shells, M4 cheekrest inlays and vent lips are authored in Blender; optic hardware is bevelled in Three.js. Door leaves are instanced across the six houses. These are original assets, with no new external art sources.

The fifth local pass sculpts continuous wooden balcony fascias and oval inlays, rounds weapon silhouette corners, tapers curved sleeve plates and adds fingerless glove colouring. The reflex hood uses an original arched profile. All geometry and material changes are authored in the included scripts; no additional external assets are used.

Actual gameplay captures and measurement output are produced locally in `graphics-qa` by `ANIME_MATCH_ONLY=1` and `ROUND_FX_ONLY=1` modes of `tools/graphics-smoke.cjs`. These use the real renderer and map assets with a local socket fixture, not a live ten-person network match. Publication remains pending owner review.
