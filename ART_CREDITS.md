# Graphics assets

Map architecture, operators, articulated gloves and weapon meshes were authored
for this project using the reproducible Blender scripts in `tools/blender`.
These assets are an original interpretation; they are not extracted Valve assets.

Surface photographs are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), under
[CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Powered by Poly Haven.
The game uses packaged files and does not call Poly Haven at runtime.

- [Plastered Stone Wall](https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_stone_wall)
- [Plastered Wall 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_wall_02)
- [Sandy Gravel](https://polyhaven.com/a/sandy_gravel)
- [Cobblestone Floor 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/cobblestone_floor_02)
- [Weathered Brown Planks](https://polyhaven.com/a/weathered_brown_planks)
- [Sandstone Blocks 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/sandstone_blocks_04)

`models/csgo/textures/sources.json` records download URLs, channels and MD5 checksums.
Scans are supplied at 1024px; the Inferno wall albedo is tinted ochre in Blender.
Other surface detail, sky and clothing textures are generated by the included scripts.
Existing assets outside `models/csgo` predate this graphics update.
