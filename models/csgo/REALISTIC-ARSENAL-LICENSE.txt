4. All remaining firearms, classic knife and karambit
   Original authored Support Hub geometry and materials, refined from the
   project's original procedural models. These are NOT imported models from
   CS, Warzone or another commercial shooter, and not renamed M4/AK models.
   Files: anime-usp, deagle, awp, scout, p90, ump45, galil, famas, sg552, aug,
   m249, fiveseven, elite, knife and karambit .glb.
   Generators: tools/blender/build_realistic_arsenal.py,
   tools/blender/build_karambit.py, with original base construction helpers.
   Improvements: neutral PBR, planar metal with manufactured edge radii,
   curved grips, continuous magazines, mechanical controls, supported optics,
   distinct compact Scout and thumbhole AWP stock, folding UMP/SG stocks,
   conservative first-person geometry and offline remote LODs.
   No exact reproduction of commercial shooter assets is asserted.

No material from the Adaptive Combat Rifle Sketchfab viewer was extracted.
Its official archive requires authenticated download and is not included.
The hands have independent provenance in their own manifest/license files.
