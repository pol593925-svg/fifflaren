Included files:
- anime-m4a1.glb: M4A1/M4A1.fbx and its original PBR texture set.
- anime-ak47.glb: AK-47/AK47.fbx and its original PBR texture set.

Support Hub modifications:
- Rebuilt missing FBX material links using the original albedo, normal,
  roughness and metallic textures.
- Converted DirectX normal orientation to OpenGL for AK-47.
- Resized normal/material maps to 1024, retained 2048 base color.
- Baked source transforms into metre-scale geometry; added grip metadata.
- Preserved separate magazine, bolt and trigger animation nodes.
- Batched static geometry and added one simplified world mesh per weapon.
- Added an original graphite dovetail/rail adapter to physically support the
  AK-47 reflex sight; the M4 sight mounts directly onto its existing rail.
- Exported embedded, self-contained glTF binary files.

The historical anime-* filenames are retained for loader compatibility;
these two files now contain the realistic CC0 replacement models.

These are NOT the Adaptive Combat Rifle by doomsentinel shown in the proposal.
That separate model's official Sketchfab download currently requires login.
No Sketchfab viewer data or assets from a commercial shooter were extracted.
